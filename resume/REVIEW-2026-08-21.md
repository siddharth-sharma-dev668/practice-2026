# Resume Review v0 — baseline (2026-08-21)

Reviewed: `SIDDHARTH_SHARMA_Resume_2026_01.pdf`

**Overall: 7/10 content, 8/10 format, 4/10 evidence.** The writing and quantification are genuinely good — better than most 1-YOE resumes. The problems are structural: no proof, some credibility risks, and positioning that lets recruiters file you under "QA."

## 🔴 Fix immediately (before any application)

1. **No GitHub. No LinkedIn.** For the roles you want, a resume without a GitHub link is a claim without evidence. Add both to the header. (The capstones in the master plan exist to make that GitHub worth clicking.)
2. **Skills section is a credibility risk.** Every line item is fair game for 10 minutes of interview probing. Can you currently defend **RocksDB, C++, SOAP, EazyBI, Hugging Face, AWS Lambda, Blockchain** under questioning? Each one you fumble poisons trust in the rest. Rule: *if you can't talk about it for 10 minutes, cut it or move it to a story you can tell.* My suggested trim keeps: Java, Python, SQL · Spring Boot, FastAPI, REST, Microservices · PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker · GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps · JUnit, TestNG, Selenium, Pytest · Power BI · MCP.
3. **Every number will be probed.** "20% latency", "10M+ messages", "40%→20% losses", "25% PR cycle time" — for each, write down (in `interview-prep/stories.md`): how it was measured, what YOU did, what tradeoff you made. A number you can't reconstruct is worse than no number.

## 🟠 Positioning (the SDET box)

- Your current title is **Automation Test Engineer (SDET)** and it's your top entry. Recruiters filter on titles. You can't change the title, but you can:
  - Lead the JCI bullets with the *engineering* (built a framework in Python/FastAPI, CI/CD integration, MCP tooling) — you mostly already do; sharpen it.
  - Make the summary say what you ARE, not what you're targeting. "Targeting roles in..." reads as *asking*. Better: "Software engineer who builds backend systems and the automation that proves they work" — owning both.
  - From W12 onward, the MCP + AI capstone work becomes your headline differentiator.
- **Job-change optics:** Onextel (5 months) → JCI looks like a hop. You have a great answer — *you interned at JCI and they brought you back*. Make that continuity visible (e.g., "Johnson Controls (returning; interned 2024)") or be ready with the one-line story.

## 🟡 Polish

- "aligning with the organisation's 2025 AI tooling roadmap" — internal jargon, means nothing outside JCI. Say what you evaluated and what you recommended.

- Projects: the IEEE project is good (keep, it's published) but it's your ONLY project. By W8 this section holds Relay; by W17 it's 3 strong entries and the college project can shrink.
