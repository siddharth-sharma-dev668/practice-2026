# Sales Playbook — how to actually increase sales

Simple model, real numbers, and a decision tree so you always know which one thing to fix next.

---

## 1. The funnel (only four numbers matter)

```
   Reach            people who saw a post
     ↓  ~2–5%
   Profile visits   people curious enough to tap your name
     ↓  ~5–15%
   Link clicks      people who tapped the link in bio
     ↓  ~2–8%
   Sales            people who paid $9
```

Multiply it through. To get **10 sales/month** at mid-range rates you need roughly:

`10 sales ÷ 4% = 250 link clicks ÷ 10% = 2,500 profile visits ÷ 3% = ~83,000 reach/month`

That is **~7,000 reach per post** at 12 posts/month. A new account gets 200–800. **This is why months 1–2 produce almost nothing** — and why one reel that hits 50k reach can outproduce your entire first two months. Volume plus consistency is how you buy lottery tickets on reach.

**The shortcut past the maths:** DMs and comments convert 10–50× better than a link in bio. A real conversation with 20 people beats 2,000 passive impressions. In month 1, talking to people *is* your growth strategy.

## 3. The decision tree — what to fix next

Work top to bottom. **Fix one thing at a time**, then wait two weeks. Changing three things at once teaches you nothing.

**Reach is low (< 500/post) →** the content is the problem, not the product.
→ Post more reels; reels out-reach static posts several-fold for small accounts. Rewrite your first lines — the hook is 80% of reach. Comment on 5 larger accounts daily.

**Reach is fine but profile visits are low →** your hook lands but your identity does not.
→ Your posts are not making people curious about *you*. Add a line of personal stake ("I failed this"). Make sure your name/handle is visible on the last slide of every carousel.

**Profile visits are fine but link clicks are low →** your bio is not doing its job.
→ Bio must say what you give and for whom, in one line, plus an explicit "↓" cue. Put the offer in the pinned post too. Never make people hunt.

**Link clicks are fine but sales are low (< 2%) →** the sales page is the problem.
→ Rewrite the top of the page (§5). Add real testimonials. Show 3 actual screenshots. Make the price and the "runs offline, your resume never leaves your device" line impossible to miss.

**Sales happen but refunds are high (> 5%) →** you over-promised.
→ Tighten the page to describe exactly what it is. Remove any implication of a guaranteed job or a guaranteed timeline.

## 4. Ten levers, in order of return per hour

1. **Reply to every comment and DM with a real sentence.** Highest-converting activity available to you, and it costs 20 minutes.
2. **Post consistently for 90 days.** Nothing else compounds. Most competitors quit at week 6; the win comes from still being there in week 12.
3. **Make the first line of every post better.** Same content, 3× the reach.
4. **Move to reels/Shorts as your primary format.** Cheapest reach for a new account.
5. **Collect a testimonial from every single buyer.** One screenshot of real feedback outperforms any copy you write.
6. **Add a checkout upsell** (Plus at $29). ~20–30% of buyers take a bigger option when it is offered at the moment of purchase. This is the single biggest revenue-per-sale lever.
7. **Build the email list.** The only audience you own. One useful email a week, no pitch.
8. **Pin your best free thing.** The diagnostic carousel does your selling for you.
9. **Show the product working in 15 seconds.** Screen recording, no narration needed.
10. **Raise the price** — only after 50 sales and real testimonials. $9 → $14 usually costs almost no conversion and adds ~50% revenue. Test it, do not guess.

**Not on this list, deliberately:** paid ads. Do not run ads until you have a page converting over 3% organically — otherwise you are paying to amplify a leak.

## 5. Sales page structure (Gumroad)

Write it in this order. Most people bury the point; do not.

1. **One-line promise** — *"An interview prep plan built around your resume, your deadline, and the hours you actually have."*
2. **The problem, in their words** — *"Every roadmap assumes 15 hours a week. You have five. So you fall behind by week 3 and quit."*
3. **What it does** — four bullets, each a concrete capability: reads your resume locally · 20-question diagnostic · generates a braided week-by-week plan · tracks progress and tells you what it had to cut.
4. **Three screenshots** — the diagnostic result with section bars, a generated week, the progress view.
5. **What it is not** — *"Not a job guarantee. Not a course. Not 400 videos you will never watch."* Naming this raises trust and cuts refunds.
6. **Privacy line** — *"Runs entirely in your browser. Your resume never leaves your device. Works offline."* This is a genuine differentiator; say it loudly.
7. **Price + what you get** — $9, one HTML file, yours forever, no subscription, no login.
8. **Refund policy** — 14 days, no questions. Cheap to honour at $9 and it removes the last hesitation.

**Objections and the honest answer:**

| They think | You say |
|---|---|
| "$9 for an HTML file?" | It is a tool, not a document. It rebuilds your plan around your diagnostic and your real hours — and works with your Wi-Fi off, forever, no subscription. |
| "Can't I just ask ChatGPT?" | You can, and it will give you a plan that assumes 15 h/week and forgets it tomorrow. This one holds your progress, quizzes you, and tells you what your hours cannot fit. |
| "Will this get me a job?" | No, and anyone promising that is lying. It tells you what to work on and keeps you honest about the hours. The work is still yours. |
| "Is my resume safe?" | It never leaves your laptop. There is no server. Turn off your Wi-Fi and it still works — try it. |

## 6. Payments and delivery

- **Gumroad** — start here. Global cards, automatic file delivery, built-in analytics, ~10% all-in. No setup cost.
- **Razorpay / Instamojo** — add when Indian buyers ask for UPI. Cheaper fees, INR pricing (₹799 reads better locally than "$9").
- **Price in both** — $9 globally, ₹799 in India. Do not just convert; ₹799 is a familiar price point and converts better than ₹749 or ₹830.
- **Delivery** — the ZIP from `dist/`. Include the README so the first thing they read tells them to open `app/index.html`.

**Test your own checkout at full price before launch.** A broken download is the most expensive bug you can ship.

## 7. What "working" looks like — decide these now

Set the bar before you have feelings invested in the outcome.

| Month | Sales | If you miss it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 (audience only) | Fine. Expected. Keep posting. |
| 2 | 10–25 | Rewrite the sales page top and the reel hooks. One change at a time. |
| 3 | 40–80 cumulative | Your format is not landing. Copy what works in your niche instead of inventing. |
| 4 | 100+ cumulative | If under 30 total by month 4 **and** your interview prep has slipped — stop and reassess honestly. |

**The real decision at month 4:** is 3 h/week better spent here, or on the prep that leads to a ₹10–25L role? Both is fine while the content *is* your study log. The moment the business starts stealing prep hours, it is costing you more than it earns.
