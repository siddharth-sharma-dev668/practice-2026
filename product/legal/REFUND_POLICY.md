# Refund policy

**14 days, no questions asked, no explanation required.** Email `<your-email@example.com>` with your order number and the refund is processed.

---

## Why it is this generous

At ₹4,999, arguing about refunds costs more than paying them. A frictionless refund also removes the last hesitation before purchase, which sells more copies than the refunds cost you. And someone who gets their money back politely will still recommend you; someone who has to fight for it will tell everyone.

## Put this on the product page

> **14-day refund, no questions asked.** If it is not useful, email me and I will refund you. You keep the file — I am not going to chase you for an HTML document.

Letting them keep the file is deliberate: it costs nothing (the file is already on their disk and cannot be recalled), and saying so out loud signals confidence.

## What to expect

Typical refund rates for a ₹4,999 digital product: **2–5%.** Above 8% means the sales page is over-promising — fix the page, not the policy. Track it in Gumroad → Analytics.

## Chargebacks

If someone files a card chargeback instead of asking you, do not fight it over ₹4,999. The fee and the time cost more than the sale. Refund, note the buyer, move on.

## One thing worth doing

When someone asks for a refund, reply with the refund **and** one question: *"Anything I could have made clearer?"* Roughly a third will answer, and those answers are the most useful product feedback you will ever get — it is the only channel where people tell you the truth about what disappointed them.
