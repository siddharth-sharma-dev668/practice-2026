LinkedIn Carousel — Week 1 launch post

8 slides, 1080×1350 (4:5 — LinkedIn's native document aspect ratio). Document/PDF-carousel posts measure ~6.6% engagement vs under 2% for plain text, so this ships as a PDF, not a wall of text.

To post: click "Export all as PDF" below, then on LinkedIn use Start a post → Add a document (not the photo icon) and upload the PDF. The caption goes in the post text box, not on a slide.
