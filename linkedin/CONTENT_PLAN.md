## Posting playbook

1. **Post 9:00–11:00 AM IST** on the scheduled day (LinkedIn India's engagement peak).
2. **The first hour decides reach**: reply to every comment within 60 min. Ask one question back per reply.
3. **Fill every `{placeholder}` with your REAL numbers before posting.** Never invent numbers — your credibility is the whole brand.
4. Comment thoughtfully on 3–5 posts in your niche daily (5 min) — this feeds your posts' reach.
5. Don't edit a post in the first hour (kills distribution). Typos can wait.
6. Reels: record on your **phone**, captions on (CapCut/InShot auto-captions), hook in the first 2 seconds, 30–45s max.

## ⚠️ Company-safety rules (read twice)

- **Post from your phone or personal device — never from the JCI laptop.**
- Never name JCI negatively, never share internal details: no project names, no screenshots of work systems, no specifics of the MCP tooling work beyond what's already public on your resume ("evaluated MCP tooling" is fine; how/for what team is not).
- Don't film in the office; blur/avoid badge logos in the Log #1 video.
- "Fortune 500 company" is the safe way to reference your employer in posts.
- Check JCI's social-media policy once before Post #1.
