# Disclaimer — read this, it is short and it is honest

**This tool will not get you a job.** Nothing will, except your own work and some luck with timing. What it does is decide *what* you work on, in what order, and how much actually fits in the hours you really have.

### What it is
- A study planner that adapts to your resume, your target role, your deadline and your honest weekly hours.
- A diagnostic that finds your real gaps instead of letting you guess.
- A progress tracker that keeps you honest week to week.

### What it is not
- **Not a job guarantee.** Anyone selling you one is lying to you.
- **Not a placement service, a referral network, or a recruiter.**
- **Not a course.** There are no video lectures. It points you at the best free primary sources — official documentation, not someone's re-explanation of it.
- **Not career, financial, legal or immigration advice.**
- **Not a substitute for actually writing code.** The plan tells you what to build. You still have to build it.

### About the interview content
Questions and topics reflect patterns common in software interviews as of **2026**, drawn from publicly available information about typical interview loops. Interview practice varies enormously by company, country, team and interviewer, and it changes over time. Treat the content as well-informed preparation material, not as a prediction of your specific interview.

Salary figures or market observations mentioned anywhere in this product are **directional context, not promises**, and they go stale quickly.

### Your data
Everything runs in your browser. Your resume is parsed **on your own device** and is never transmitted anywhere — there is no server, no account, no telemetry, no analytics. You can verify this: turn off your Wi-Fi and the whole tool still works.

Your progress is stored in your browser's local storage. That means:
- Clearing your browser data will erase it.
- It does not sync between devices or browsers.
- **Use Progress → Export regularly** if your progress matters to you. That is the only backup that exists.
